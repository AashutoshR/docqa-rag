# Brightleaf Labs Employee Handbook

Brightleaf Labs is a fictional company. This handbook is sample data for the DocQA project.

## Working hours

Core hours are 10:00 to 16:00, Monday to Friday. Outside core hours, employees choose when to work as long as they complete 40 hours per week. Meetings should not be scheduled before 10:00 or after 16:00 without the agreement of everyone invited.

## Remote work

Employees may work remotely up to three days per week. Tuesday is the company-wide in-office day and everyone based within 50 km of an office is expected to attend. Fully remote arrangements require written approval from the department head and are reviewed every 12 months.

## Annual leave

Full-time employees receive 24 days of paid annual leave per calendar year, in addition to public holidays. Up to 5 unused days may be carried over into the next year and must be used by 31 March, after which they are forfeited. Leave requests of more than 5 consecutive days must be submitted at least 4 weeks in advance through the HR portal.

## Sick leave

Employees receive 10 days of paid sick leave per year. A medical certificate is required for absences longer than 3 consecutive working days. Sick leave does not carry over.

## Parental leave

Primary caregivers are entitled to 20 weeks of fully paid parental leave. Secondary caregivers are entitled to 6 weeks of fully paid leave, which must be taken within the first year after the birth or adoption.

## Expenses

Business expenses are reimbursed through the Finch expenses tool. Claims must be submitted within 30 days of the purchase and include a receipt. Any single expense above 500 USD needs manager approval before the purchase is made. Meals while travelling are reimbursed up to 60 USD per day.

## Learning budget

Each employee has an annual learning budget of 1,500 USD for courses, books and conferences. The budget resets on 1 January and unused amounts do not carry over. Conference travel is paid from the team travel budget, not the learning budget.

## Performance reviews

Performance reviews take place twice a year, in June and December. Salary adjustments are decided after the December review and take effect on 1 February.

## Probation and notice

New hires have a probation period of 3 months. During probation, either side may end the employment with 2 weeks of notice. After probation, the notice period is 2 months.
