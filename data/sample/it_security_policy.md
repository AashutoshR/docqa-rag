# Brightleaf Labs IT Security Policy

Brightleaf Labs is a fictional company. This policy is sample data for the DocQA project.

## Passwords

Passwords must be at least 14 characters long. All employees must store work passwords in the company password manager, Vaultline. Passwords are not rotated on a schedule; they must be changed immediately if a breach is suspected. Sharing passwords over chat or email is prohibited.

## Multi-factor authentication

Multi-factor authentication is mandatory for email, the VPN, source control and all systems holding customer data. Hardware security keys are the required second factor for engineers and administrators. Other staff may use an authenticator app. SMS codes are not an accepted second factor.

## Devices

Company laptops must have full-disk encryption enabled and lock automatically after 5 minutes of inactivity. Operating system security updates must be installed within 7 days of release. Personal devices may be used for email and chat only if they are enrolled in mobile device management.

## Lost or stolen devices

A lost or stolen device must be reported to the IT helpdesk within 1 hour of discovery, at any time of day. IT will remotely wipe the device. Replacement laptops are normally issued within 2 working days.

## Data classification

Data is classified as Public, Internal, Confidential or Restricted. Customer personal data is always Restricted. Restricted data may only be stored in approved systems and must never be copied to personal devices, personal cloud storage or removable media.

## Security incidents

Suspected security incidents must be reported to security@brightleaf.example or the #security-incidents channel as soon as they are noticed. The security team acknowledges reports within 30 minutes during business hours. Incidents involving customer data are escalated to the Data Protection Officer, who decides whether regulators must be notified within 72 hours.

## Access reviews

Managers review their team's system access every quarter. Access for departing employees is revoked on their last working day. Administrator access is granted for a maximum of 90 days and must then be renewed.

## Training

All employees complete security awareness training within their first 2 weeks and again every 12 months. Simulated phishing exercises are run monthly.
